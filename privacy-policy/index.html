Khmerlish Keyboard · Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026

ក្តារចុចមោទនភាពខ្មែរ (“Khmerlish Keyboard”) is an Android keyboard that converts Khmerlish typing into Khmer text on your device.

The app is developed and published by Cheng Sokdara.

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share typed content, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, analytics events, crash reports, or advertising data.

The Khmerlish lexicon is bundled with the app, and suggestions are generated locally on your device.

Personal Dictionary

If you use My Dictionary or teach the keyboard a spelling, the app stores the Khmerlish alias and Khmer word locally on your device. This information is used only to improve suggestions on that device.

Personal Dictionary data is not uploaded to the developer or any third party. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app, so these entries are not backed up by the app.

Secure fields

In password and other secure fields, the keyboard types direct ASCII, hides Khmer suggestions, and does not learn entries.

Data retention and deletion

Personal Dictionary entries and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. The developer has no server copy to retain or delete.

Children

The app is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

Changes

If the app later adds optional sync, analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or any network feature, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email chengsokdara@gmail.com.

Khmerlish Keyboard processes suggestions on-device and does not transmit what you type.
