Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

ក្តារចុចមោទនភាពខ្មែរ (“Khmer Pride Keyboard”) is an Android keyboard that converts familiar Latin-letter typing into Khmer text on your device.

The app is developed and published by Cheng Sokdara.

This policy covers:

  • The free keyboard app com.chengsokdara.keyboard
  • The optional paid companion com.chengsokdara.keyboard.pro (“Khmer Pride Keyboard Pro”)

It applies to versions of the free keyboard that include host-app ads and Firebase Analytics (the Play release after 0.6.0 / version code 10). Builds 0.5.0 and 0.6.0 do not include those SDKs.

What we never collect

The keyboard does not send what you type, password fields, messages, suggestions you see, or My Dictionary words to us or to ad networks.

Suggestions run on your device from a bundled word list. The IME (the keyboard itself) does not make analytics or ad requests while you type.

Data the free keyboard may collect (host app)

When you open the host app (Setup, Learn, Practice, My Dictionary, Settings, Tutorial), the free keyboard may use the network for:

Analytics (Firebase Analytics, Google)

We use Firebase Analytics to see whether people open the app, finish setup, use the tutorial, and whether the keyboard was used that day.

Typical data:

  • App opens and which host screen you opened
  • Tutorial step, skip, and finish
  • Whether the keyboard is enabled and selected
  • Whether Pro is installed
  • A once-per-day “keyboard used today” signal (timestamp and layout name such as Latin-to-Khmer / Khmer / English — not the text you typed)
  • App instance identifiers used by Firebase

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The free host app may show ads on Setup, Tutorial replay (steps 3–5), and My Dictionary. Ads are not shown on the first-run tutorial or on the keyboard while you type.

We use Unity LevelPlay as the mediator. Current bidding networks:

  • Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms)
  • Liftoff Monetize (formerly Vungle)
  • ironSource Ads

Mintegral may be added later after their account review. This policy and the Google Play Data Safety form will list Mintegral only when that network is actually in the app.

Ad networks may receive:

  • Your device’s Advertising ID
  • Device and app signals needed to request and measure ads
  • Ad impressions and approximate revenue events

They do not receive the text you type or My Dictionary entries.

Installing Khmer Pride Keyboard Pro removes these host-app ads. Pro users are not shown host ads.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we use Google’s UMP consent form before personalized ads.

7-day Pro trial (host app)

When you start or continue the 7-day Pro trial, the host app may contact https://khmerlish-keyboard-community.vercel.app/ over HTTPS with:

  • A Play Integrity token (so we can confirm the request comes from this app on this device)
  • A hashed Android device id (SHA-256 of ANDROID_ID). This is a device identifier, not an account. We do not receive or store your Google account, email, or name for the trial.

This check exists so uninstall on the same Android profile cannot mint a new 7-day trial. Factory reset, another user profile, a sideloaded build, and a new phone can still start a new trial. We do not create a user account. The IME does not send this request while you type.

Permissions

The free app may request network access and the Advertising ID. It does not request location for ads.

Personal Dictionary

If you use My Dictionary or teach the keyboard a spelling, the app stores the Latin spelling and Khmer word locally on your device. This information is used to improve suggestions on that device.

Local only (default)

By default, personal dictionary data is not uploaded to the developer. Android Auto Backup is limited to a small Pro-trial clock file (trial timestamps and a hashed device id) so uninstalling does not reset a used 7-day trial when Google backup is enabled. My Dictionary, typing preferences, and other app data are excluded from Android backup.

Optional Pro cloud backup (Google Drive)

Cloud backup runs in the Pro companion app, not in the keyboard process. If you install Pro and choose Connect Google Drive, that companion app may:

  1. Ask you to sign in with Google and grant access to the app’s private Drive App Data folder (not your full Drive files).
  2. Upload and download a backup of My Dictionary entries to that folder in your Google account.

We do not host your My Dictionary on our own servers. The backup lives in your Google account. You can disconnect in the app, delete the cloud backup from the app, or revoke app access in your Google Account settings. Local words on the device are kept when you disconnect unless you delete them yourself.

Typing you do in other apps is not uploaded as part of cloud backup.

Accounts and purchases

The free keyboard does not create a developer username or password. The 7-day Pro trial does not register an account.

Google Sign-In is optional and used only for Drive App Data cloud backup in the Pro companion. Sign-in does not unlock Pro. Pro is a separate paid Play Store app (com.chengsokdara.keyboard.pro). Installing it unlocks Pro. ABA bank tips never unlock features or cloud sync.

Secure fields

In password and other secure fields, the keyboard types direct ASCII, hides Khmer suggestions, and does not learn entries.

Sharing

We do not sell your data.

We share the analytics and advertising data described above with:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics)
  • Unity LevelPlay, including ironSource Ads
  • Meta Platforms (Audience Network)
  • Liftoff (Monetize)

Those companies process the data under their own policies to provide analytics or ads. Pro Drive backup is stored in your Google account, not shared with ad networks.

Retention and deletion

Local dictionary and preferences stay on the device until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall.

Cloud dictionary backup is deleted when you use Delete cloud backup or remove app access in Google Account settings. See delete data and delete account.

Analytics events are not stored as a named account we can look up. Uninstalling the app stops new events from this install. You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in Android Settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If we add a new ad network (including Mintegral), crash reporting, or another network destination, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety form will be updated before that release.
